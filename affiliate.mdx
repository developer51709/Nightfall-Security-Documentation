The Nightfall Affiliate Programme lets approved partners earn recognition and perks by referring Discord servers to the bot's premium tier. Affiliates share a unique code; every server that redeems the code starts a 7‑day free trial, and servers that convert to paid Premium count toward the affiliate's tier.

---

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your code">
    Affiliate codes are issued by Nightfall bot administrators. Once approved, you receive a unique code to share.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share your code">
    Share your code with Discord server administrators. They redeem it with:

    ```
    /premium redeem code:YOUR-AFFILIATE-CODE
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trial activates">
    The server receives a **7‑day free trial** of Nightfall Premium immediately. Each server can only redeem one affiliate trial ever.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track conversions">
    Monitor your trials, conversions, and tier progress with `/affiliate stats`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Earn perks">
    As your conversions accumulate, you advance through tiers and unlock recognition, priority support, and payout eligibility.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tier system

| Tier | Conversions required | Payout eligible | Perks |
|------|---------------------|:---------------:|-------|
| **Partner** | 0 | ❌ | Start tracking — share your code and earn conversions |
| **Bronze** | 1+ | ❌ | Affiliate badge in `/affiliate stats` |
| **Silver** | 3+ | ❌ | Priority support mention + custom analytics |
| **Gold** | 7+ | ✅ | Revenue‑share eligibility + payout wallet + priority support |
| **Diamond** | 15+ | ✅ | Partner spotlight eligibility + all Gold perks |

Tiers are calculated live from your conversion count — no manual tier assignment is needed.

## Analytics

View your full analytics dashboard with:

```
/affiliate stats
```

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Total trials | Guilds that redeemed your code |
| Active trials | Trials currently running |
| Pending conversions | Trials expired, discount sent, awaiting purchase |
| Conversions | Guilds that purchased Premium after the trial |
| Conversion rate | `conversions ÷ total_trials × 100` |

---

## Payout system

Affiliates at **Gold tier or above** (7+ conversions) may register a crypto wallet for payouts.

```
/affiliate payout
```

Use the **Set / Update Wallet** button in the panel to enter your wallet address and network. Supported networks include Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), USDT‑TRC20, USDT‑ERC20, USDC‑ERC20, Litecoin (LTC), and others.

Payouts are processed by the Nightfall team on a rolling schedule for eligible affiliates.

---

## Post‑trial discount

When a trial expires without the server converting to paid Premium, Nightfall automatically sends the server owner a **one‑time DM** with a discount offer (20% off the first month). If the server owner later purchases, a conversion is credited to your account.

## Becoming an affiliate

Affiliate codes are issued by registered Nightfall bot administrators. To apply:

1. Join the [official support server](https://discord.gg/CXzX38Z7J9)
2. Contact the team to express interest
3. If approved, your code is activated immediately
4. Reach **Gold tier** (7+ conversions) to unlock payout registration

---

## Rules

- **Accurate representation** — affiliates must represent Nightfall and the trial offer honestly. Misleading or deceptive promotion is prohibited.
- **No fraudulent activity** — creating fake trial redemptions, coordinating false conversions, or any other affiliate fraud results in immediate removal and cancellation of pending perks.
- **Code ownership** — affiliate codes remain the property of Nightfall bot administrators and may be revoked at any time.
