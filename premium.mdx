# Nightfall Premium

Nightfall Premium enhances your server with advanced detection, improved automation, and priority processing.

## Pricing

Nightfall Premium costs **$6.00/month**.  
Payments are handled through **OxaPay** (crypto).

## Premium Commands

- `/premium buy`
- `/premium status`
- `/premium cancel`
- `/premium customize`

## Premium Benefits

- Faster Anti‑Raid detection  
- Enhanced Anti‑Nuke thresholds  
- Priority event processing  
- Extended AutoMod rule limits  
- Premium verification modes  
- Additional logging categories  

Premium is optional — Nightfall’s core protections remain free.
