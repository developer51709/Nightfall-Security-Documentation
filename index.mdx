Nightfall Security is a professional, multi‑layer protection system engineered to safeguard Discord communities of every size. Whether you manage a gaming group, development team, creator community, educational hub, or a private administrative space, Nightfall provides reliable, policy‑aligned security designed to keep your server safe.

Nightfall integrates advanced systems such as **Anti‑Nuke**, **Anti‑Raid**, **AutoMod**, and **Verification** to detect destructive actions, block malicious behaviour, filter unsafe content, and prevent unauthorised access. Its resilient multi‑process architecture ensures stability, uptime, and consistent protection — even under heavy load.

## Security model

All features are **strictly defensive**:

- ✅ Detect, log, and alert on suspicious behaviour
- ✅ Engage configurable responses to pause destructive‑action tracking
- ✅ Notify administrators via a dedicated alert channel
- ❌ No automated offensive actions against other servers or users
- ❌ No tools for raiding, nuking, or harming communities
